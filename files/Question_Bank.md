# Amazon PM-T Question Bank

> **Purpose**: Master question bank with LP primary/secondary mapping, strength/concern signals, and recommended Vinayak story for each.
> Companion to: `LP_DeepDive.md` (definitions + GenAI Fluency rubric), `Candidate_Profile_Master.md` (story bank).

---

## How to use this doc

Each question carries:
- **Primary LP** — what they're really testing
- **Secondary LP** — what shows up in follow-ups (same answer, different angle)
- **⭐ Recommended story** — your strongest match from `Candidate_Profile_Master.md` § 7
- **✅ Hit signals** — behaviors the answer must demonstrate
- **❌ Avoid signals** — behaviors that flag "no hire"

When practicing: pick a Q → say the story aloud → check the signals → adjust. The story bank in `Candidate_Profile_Master.md` is the source of truth; this doc tells you which story fits which Q and what to emphasize.

**Story numbering**: refers to Candidate_Profile_Master.md § 7 STAR Story Bank table (1–22).

## Tier 0: Universal questions (asked across every Amazon role)

These show up regardless of LP focus. Have rock-solid answers.

### Q: Why Amazon?
- **Probes**: motivation, fit, knowledge of company
- **⭐ Story**: not story-driven — needs a 60-90 second prepared answer connecting your trajectory (CS → SWE → PM → MSIS in ML/AI) to Amazon's PM-T role specifically. Reference 2–3 LPs you most resonate with (Customer Obsession, Invent and Simplify, Learn and Be Curious are honest fits).
- **✅ Hit**: shows you understand PM-T role specifically (not "I love Amazon"); ties to your tech-product trajectory; references real Amazon products/teams/strategy
- **❌ Avoid**: prestige-seeking; generic "innovative culture"; mentioning compensation; not differentiating Amazon from FAANG peers

### Q: Tell me about a time you failed at work. What did you learn?
- **Primary**: Ownership / Learn and Be Curious
- **⭐ Story**: needs writing. Best raw material: deadline-miscommit on a Zynga launch, OR Vinsight architectural choice that didn't work and you pivoted. *(See LP_DeepDive.md sample answer for the calibration target.)*
- **✅ Hit**: real failure (not "I worked too hard"); own it personally; structural lesson (not platitude)
- **❌ Avoid**: blaming team / external factors; framing weakness as strength

### Q: Tell me about a challenge you faced. What was your role and outcome?
- **Primary**: Deliver Results / Ownership
- **⭐ Story**: #12 Dialogue Palghar forum (real cross-stakeholder challenge with no formal authority)
- **✅ Hit**: scope/complexity made explicit; your specific contribution; quantified outcome
- **❌ Avoid**: team-level credit ("we"); under-scoping the difficulty

### Q: Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker / manager / decision.
- **Primary**: Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
- **⭐ Story**: ⚠️ **GAP** — needs writing. See LP_DeepDive.md § 13 for what a strong Backbone story looks like.
- **✅ Hit**: respectful pushback with data; showed conviction; once decided, committed wholly even if it went against you
- **❌ Avoid**: bulldozing; "I won the argument"; passive aggression; backing down without conviction

### Q: Tell me about a time you had to work under a tight deadline / make a quick decision.
- **Primary**: Bias for Action
- **⭐ Story**: #13 CM Fellow COVID war-room SOPs (genuine urgency, calculated decisions with incomplete info)
- **✅ Hit**: urgency real; you chose action over analysis paralysis; outcome positive even if imperfect
- **❌ Avoid**: reckless ("I just did it"); no consideration of risk
